# Privacy policy: Lexio Rent – Fleet & Bookings (browser extension)

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This extension is published by Lexio Rent (contact@lexio.rent), the company behind the [Lexio Rent car rental software](https://lexio.rent/en/car-rental-software).

## What the extension stores

- **Your Lexio API key**, which you type in the settings page, and the name of your Lexio organization. Both are kept in the extension's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) in your browser profile. They are not synced and not sent anywhere else.

## What the extension sends, and where

- The extension sends requests only to `https://api.lexio.rent`, the Lexio Rent API, with your key, to read your own data: vehicles and their availability and prices, rentals, and customers looked up by email or rental number.
- It sends nothing to Lexio Rent or to anyone else: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party scripts.
- It does not read the pages you visit, your browsing history, your tabs or your cookies. Its only browser permission is `storage`, and its only host permission is `https://api.lexio.rent/*`.

## Data shown

Rental and customer data displayed in the popup comes from your Lexio organization and stays in the popup. It is not cached by the extension. The processing of that data in Lexio is covered by the [Lexio privacy policy](https://lexio.rent/politique-de-confidentialite) (in French).

## Removing your data

Click **Remove key** in the extension settings, or uninstall the extension: both delete the stored key. You can also revoke the key at any time in Lexio, under Settings › Developer › API tokens.
